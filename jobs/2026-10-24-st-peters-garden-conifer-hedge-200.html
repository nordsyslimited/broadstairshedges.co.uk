Big Garden Conifer Hedge Trimmed Back for £200, Near St Peters

There is a particular kind of hedge that people put off. It is big, it is at the bottom of the garden, and it has got a bit more out of hand every year. Eventually it is simply on the list of things that will get done one day. This one, near St Peters, was exactly that, and we trimmed the sides and the top for £200.

The job

A big conifer hedge at the end of a garden, with the sides and top due a cut. A trim at the right time keeps a job like this simple and affordable, before it turns into a harder and more expensive one.

Why the top is the hard part

When people say they have been putting off the hedge, what they usually mean is the top. Sides are easy, since you can see them and reach them from the ground. The top is another matter. It is out of sight, it is high up, and it is where most of the effort and most of the risk sits. That is the part we are for.

What you get for £200

Sides and top, trimmed back and shaped to a clean line. A fixed price agreed before any cutting begins. And if it turns out to be a bigger job than the photo suggested, we say so first and quote again, so there is never a surprise bill.

Getting a price from a photo

Most hedges can be priced from a photo. If you are thinking about getting a hedge done, send us a picture of the whole run, one from further back so we can see the height, and tell us roughly how long it is. We will come back with a fixed price. If the hedge is smaller than this one, the price will be lower; if it is bigger, it will be higher, and either way you will know before we start.

The result

The lawn, the borders and the patio all look different with the hedge back in line. It lets in more light and the whole garden looks bigger. The photo shows the finished job.

Hedge work across Thanet
