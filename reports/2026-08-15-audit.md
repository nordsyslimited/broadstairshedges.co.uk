### NR-2. HTML entity in JSON-LD headline on two articles

`&amp;` appears in the `"headline"` field of JSON-LD `Article` objects. JSON-LD is parsed as JSON, not HTML; the entity renders as the literal string `&amp;` rather than `&`.

| File | Affected field |
|------|---------------|
| `how-to/high-hedges-neighbour-law-broadstairs.html` | `"headline": "High Hedges, Neighbour Law, and the Broadstairs £650 Fee &amp; What to Do"` (or similar) |
| `how-to/dickens-festival-holiday-let-hedge-tidy-broadstairs.html` | headline field contains `&amp;` |

**Recommended fix:** Replace `&amp;` with `&` inside the JSON-LD `<script>` blocks only (leave HTML attributes untouched). Judgment call on exact headline text needed.
