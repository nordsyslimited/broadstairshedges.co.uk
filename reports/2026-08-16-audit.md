### JSON-LD `&amp;` entity fixed in 2 articles

In `<script type="application/ld+json">` blocks, HTML entities are not decoded by the
JSON parser. The `"headline"` field contained the literal string `&amp;` rather than
`&`. Fixed by replacing with the unencoded ampersand in both JSON strings.

- `how-to/high-hedges-neighbour-law-broadstairs.html` — headline now: `High Hedges & Neighbour Law in Broadstairs: TDC's £650 Complaint Fee`
- `how-to/dickens-festival-holiday-let-hedge-tidy-broadstairs.html` — headline now: `Dickens Festival & Holiday-Let Hedge Tidy in Broadstairs`

Note: `&amp;` in HTML attribute values (e.g. `og:title content="..."`) is correct HTML
and was not changed.
