Portuguese laurel is a distinct species from cherry laurel (Prunus laurocerasus): smaller leaves (60-100mm vs up to 200mm), red stems, slower growth rate (20-40cm/yr vs 60cm/yr), meaningfully better drought tolerance on Margate chalk, and better salt-wind performance at exposed coastal positions. It is increasingly chosen in CT10 gardens over cherry laurel precisely because it handles Thanet conditions more reliably. The existing cherry-laurel article does not cover it. Topic fills a genuine species gap in the pruning category and addresses a question frequently arising in coastal Broadstairs/St Peter's garden contexts.
