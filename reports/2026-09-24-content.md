## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity

- Margate Chalk noted: free-draining, alkaline soil means slit trench on CT10 can dry out faster than clay sites; horticultural sand layer in trench base specifically recommended for CT10 chalk conditions

- Coastal winter advantage: mild maritime winters on CT10 (mean January temperature 3.5-4 degrees Celsius, frosts below -7 degrees Celsius rare) make outdoor slit-trench propagation viable without cold-frame protection, unlike colder parts of the UK
