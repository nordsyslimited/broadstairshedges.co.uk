This run fills a genuine gap within "Planting technique on Margate Chalk": specifically root ball hedge planting as a distinct method with its own handling requirements, soil preparation technique and watering regime. The gap was confirmed absent across all 52 existing slugs: no slug or article title contains "root ball" or "rootball" as a primary topic. The bare-root-hedge-planting-broadstairs.html article covers bare-root planting in full but explicitly notes it is a different category of plant stock from root ball or pot-grown. The differences are substantive:

- Root ball plants are field-grown for 3-5 years, lifted with a solid soil ball wrapped in biodegradable hessian, available Oct-April only
- The hessian wrapping is left in place (not removed as bare-root roots would be), requiring specific technique
- Root balls at 1.0-1.5m height give instant visual impact; bare-root is 40-60cm at planting
- Weight and access requirements are substantially different (20-40 kg per plant vs a few hundred grams for bare-root)
- The watering demand in the first summer is much higher for root ball plants because of their greater leaf area
- The chalk-depth survey step before ordering is specific to root ball (root balls cannot be planted where chalk is shallower than the root ball depth)

The October timing for this article is also highly appropriate: root ball planting season begins in October and the article addresses the genuine need of CT10 property owners considering a new hedge for the 2026-27 planting season.
