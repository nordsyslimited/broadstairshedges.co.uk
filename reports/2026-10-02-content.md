Existing articles cover 54 how-to slugs. A full review of the slug list against `config/ingestion.json` categories and the existing hub grid confirmed no article existed for Hebe (shrubby veronica) pruning.

Hebe is among the most commonly grown salt-tolerant evergreens in Broadstairs, St Peter's and the terrace gardens above Viking Bay. It appears throughout CT10 gardens as a boundary hedge or specimen plant. Despite this prevalence, the site had no procedural guide on pruning it, which is a genuine and exploitable content gap.

The topic also carries a non-trivial procedural hook that differentiates the article from generic hebe content: the "old-wood rule" (Hebe does not regenerate from leafless old wood, unlike yew, cherry laurel or box) is the most common cause of pruning failure on this species in UK gardens, and framing the article around this rule first produces a genuinely useful procedural guide. It also maps directly to this site's procedural-first structural angle.
