## WCA 1981 callout

Callout placed after Step 2 (nest check). Notes that the post-flowering cut for Japanese spiraea (July-August) and spring-flowering types (late May-June) both fall within the 1 March-31 August nesting window. Identifies the early-spring renovation cut (February-March) as the one window outside the nesting season. Penalty cited as per the existing site convention (unlimited fine per nest).
