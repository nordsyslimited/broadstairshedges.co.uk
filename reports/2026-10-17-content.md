## Topic discovery

### Gap analysis

Reviewed all 67 existing `how-to/` articles (excluding index.html and redirect stub) against the nine category list in `config/ingestion.json` and the category tracking in `reports/status.json`. The clearest uncovered gap is **how to establish boundary hedge ownership**. The "Neighbour law and high-hedge disputes" category currently has only one article (`high-hedges-neighbour-law-broadstairs.html`). The boundary ownership question is procedurally distinct from the high-hedges complaint process: high-hedges is about formal council complaints against a neighbour's hedge; boundary ownership is about how to establish which hedge is yours in the first place, before any cutting, renovation or removal work.

No existing article covers:
- How to download and read the Land Registry title plan
- What T marks and H marks on deed plans mean
- The hedge-and-ditch common-law presumption and its practical application in Kent
- How to cross-reference a neighbouring property's title documents
- The myths around left-side rules and maintenance-creates-ownership
- The step from owner identification to formal boundary dispute resolution

This is a genuine procedural gap frequently raised by CT10 homeowners before commissioning hedge work, and the first topic they need to resolve before any of the cutting/renovation/reduction articles become actionable.

Category fit: "Neighbour law and high-hedge disputes". Strong Broadstairs/Thanet specificity available: Victorian terrace rows in Broadstairs CA, St Peter's CA boundary hedge context, cliff-side Griselinia boundaries above Stone Bay and Joss Bay, Reading Street and older field boundaries where the hedge-and-ditch presumption applies.
