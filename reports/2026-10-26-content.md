## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity

The article incorporates the following localising detail:

- **Margate Chalk Formation:** alkaline (pH 7.5-8.0), fast-draining, thin topsoil 200-300mm over solid chalk. Sea buckthorn is nitrogen-fixing and establishes on very low-nutrient chalk soils without supplementary fertiliser: noted as a reason the species is well-suited to CT10 coastal gardens where other hedging species struggle.
- **Thanet Coast SSSI:** the SSSI designation covers the foreshore and cliff face from Minnis Bay to Ramsgate. Sea buckthorn within the SSSI boundary forms a designated coastal dune thicket habitat; garden hedges in the domestic curtilage are not subject to SSSI consent, but the MAGIC map reference is included for owners uncertain about their boundary.
- **North Foreland and Kingsgate Bay positions:** named as clifftop garden locations where sea buckthorn is managed and where salt-wind exposure makes it a rational windbreak choice.
- **Fieldfare and redwing arrivals:** Scandinavian winter visitors arriving in October on the Thanet coast to feed on the orange berry crop; a seasonal anchor unique to CT10 coastal gardens.
- **Root barrier on Margate Chalk:** specific advice for installing a 500mm HDPE root barrier to contain suckering on chalk-over-shingle soil profile.
