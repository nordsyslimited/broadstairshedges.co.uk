## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity

The article incorporates the following localising detail:

- **Margate Chalk Formation:** alkaline (pH 7.5-8.0), fast-draining; elder is a pioneer species on chalk soils, grows vigorously on thin chalk without supplementary fertiliser, reaches 600-1,000mm of new growth per season in CT10 garden conditions.
- **Seasonal anchor:** fieldfare and redwing arrival on the Thanet coast in October; elderberry harvest stripped by early November; coppice window opens naturally after the berry harvest clears.
- **Named locations:** Broadstairs, St Peter's, Reading Street fringe, Viking Bay (fieldfare arrival reference), Poorhole Lane Thanet DC household waste recycling centre for disposal.
- **CT10 hedge character:** elder arrival as a pioneer in established hawthorn/blackthorn hedges; colonises chalk banks and cliff margins; Reading Street road fringe hedgerows named.
- **Conservation area detail:** four named CAs (Broadstairs CA, St Peter's CA, Reading Street CA, Kingsgate CA), section 211 TCPA 1990 threshold (75mm at 1.5m), TDC notification requirement for standalone elder specimens.
- **Nesting species:** blackbird, robin, blackcap, whitethroat, house sparrow; blackcap (Sylvia atricapilla) named as a regular elder-nesting species in Kent.
