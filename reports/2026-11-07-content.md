**Topic identified:** Brachyglottis (Dunedin Group) 'Sunshine' — spring pruning method and the bare-old-wood rule.

**Gap rationale:** A survey of all existing how-to articles confirmed no article covering this species existed. Brachyglottis 'Sunshine' is one of the most widely planted silver-foliage shrubs in Broadstairs and Kingsgate front gardens, well-suited to Margate chalk and Thanet salt-wind exposure. It is not mentioned in the existing coastal species guide (coastal-hedges-broadstairs-species-guide.html). The pruning requirement — an annual mid-spring cut, never into bare old wood — is specific, frequently misunderstood and directly analogous to the site's lavender and hebe articles, which are among the more-linked guides in the existing set.
