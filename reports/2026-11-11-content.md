## Topic discovery

### Gap analysis

Reviewed all 93 existing `how-to/` articles (as of 2026-11-10) against the nine-category list in `config/ingestion.json`. No existing slug covered Prunus cerasifera (myrobalan / cherry plum) as a subject.

This fills a genuine gap for two reasons:

1. **Counter-intuitive timing rule.** Cherry plum must be pruned in summer, not in autumn or winter, because of silver leaf (Chondrostereum purpureum) fungal infection risk. This makes it procedurally distinct from every other deciduous hedging species covered on this site. All other native deciduous hedging articles (hawthorn, blackthorn, hazel, field maple, hornbeam, beech, elder, guelder rose) recommend winter or dormant-season cutting. Myrobalan is the single species in the CT10 hedgerow palette where the reverse applies. That distinction earns a dedicated article.

2. **Local distribution and mismanagement risk.** Prunus cerasifera is widely planted in CT10 as green-leaved hedging in Reading Street, North Foreland and St Peter's lanes, and as purple-leaved ornamental boundary hedging ('Pissardii', 'Nigra') in the Victorian and Edwardian residential streets behind Viking Bay and within the Broadstairs conservation area. Many owners cut it at the wrong time of year because hedge-cutting season is perceived as an autumn/winter activity. An article correcting this helps local readers avoid a damaging and potentially irreversible mistake.

## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity

The article incorporates:

- **Green-leaved vs purple-leaved distribution:** green myrobalan most common in Reading Street, North Foreland roadlines and larger St Peter's gardens; purple-leaved 'Pissardii' and 'Nigra' most common in Victorian/Edwardian residential streets behind Viking Bay and in the Broadstairs conservation area
- **Margate Chalk:** alkaline (pH 7.5-8.0), fast-draining; Prunus cerasifera noted as tolerant of these conditions relative to beech or hornbeam
- **Annual rainfall:** approximately 615mm across Thanet, cited in the context of establishment watering for chalk soils
- **Four conservation areas** (Broadstairs CA, St Peter's CA, Reading Street CA, Kingsgate CA) noted; section 211 TCPA 1990 threshold discussed and practical guidance given that routine pruning does not normally trigger a notice requirement
- **Timing conflict of summer pruning vs nesting season:** the entire recommended pruning window (late May to late July) falls inside the WCA 1981 guidance period. This is highlighted in the callout and throughout Step 2 and Step 3
