## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity notes

- Margate Chalk compatibility confirmed: mahonia is chalk/alkaline tolerant, does not need soil amendment, one of the more reliable evergreen shrubs for thin CT10 chalk soils.
- Drought tolerance noted with specific Manston rainfall figure (615mm/yr).
- Salt-wind position: moderate tolerance; suitable for sheltered and semi-sheltered positions but not front-line clifftop without a windbreak species in front.
- Species prevalence: very common in Broadstairs and St Peter's front gardens; north-facing boundary planting noted.
- Wildlife value at the CT10 seasonal level: post-winter flowers (February-April) for early-emerging queen bumblebees; berries for blackbirds and thrushes; cover structure for wrens and dunnocks.
- Conservation area note included: mahonia as a low shrub does not normally trigger s.211, but a large multi-stem specimen could reach the 75mm threshold.
