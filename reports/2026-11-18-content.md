## Topic discovery

**Topic identified:** Ash dieback (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus / Chalara fraxinea) in CT10 hedgerows: how to survey, identify and assess affected ash trees, categorise by severity, carry out nesting-law-compliant felling of dangerous trees, and replant gaps with chalk-tolerant native species.

**Gap rationale:** A survey of all 100 existing how-to articles confirmed no article covering ash dieback existed. The existing disease/pest articles cover box blight (Cylindrocladium buxicola), box tree moth (Cydalima perspectalis), fireblight (Erwinia amylovora), honey fungus (Armillaria spp.), vine weevil (Otiorhynchus sulcatus), scale insects, and aphids. None addresses the most significant tree disease affecting UK hedgerows since Dutch elm disease: ash dieback. Ash (Fraxinus excelsior) is a major hedgerow tree in Thanet and east Kent; Kent County Council has had a formal ash dieback action plan since 2013, and the disease is now confirmed as affecting ash trees across east Kent woodland and hedgerow contexts. The procedural gap: no article on this site addresses (a) the correct survey window (late July to September) and why it matters, (b) the diagnostic distinction between diseased and normal dieback, (c) the management categorisation framework (monitor/watch/plan for removal), or (d) the legal clearances required before felling significant ash in CT10 (conservation areas, TPOs, Hedgerow Regulations). Category covered: Pests & disease. The topic also touches Planting technique on Margate Chalk (category 8) through the replacement species section, Conservation-area and section-211 paperwork (category 5), and Wildlife & the law through the nesting-season callout.

## Broadstairs/Thanet specificity notes

- Ash confirmed as a hedgerow tree in Thanet district, particularly on the rural margins of Broadstairs (Reading Street fringe, North Foreland area), in older garden boundaries and along public footpaths in CT10.
- Margate Chalk replacement species: field maple, hawthorn, wild cherry, spindle, hornbeam, dogwood, hazel selected specifically for chalk-soil compatibility and native hedgerow value.
- Four conservation areas (Broadstairs CA, St Peter's CA, Reading Street CA, Kingsgate CA) flagged with s.211 notice requirement; Thanet District Council named as the authority for TDC TPO check and hedgerow regulations submission.
